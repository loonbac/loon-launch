# Third-party assets

## Header images (`assets/headers/`)

**Origin.** The 201 images in `assets/headers/` come from
[`DerpFest-AOSP/packages_apps_OmniStyle`](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/packages_apps_OmniStyle),
branch `15`, directory
[`res/drawable-nodpi`](https://github.com/DerpFest-AOSP/packages_apps_OmniStyle/tree/15/res/drawable-nodpi).

**License: none declared.** That upstream repository contains **no LICENSE
file** and no per-file license or copyright header on the artwork, so the images
are *not* explicitly licensed by their authors. They are redistributed here on
the assumption of fair, non-commercial personal use, and this repository makes
no claim of ownership over them. This is a known and accepted risk for local
use; it must be resolved before any public or commercial distribution of this
package.

**Local modifications.** The files are not byte-identical to upstream: WebP
images were converted to JPEG (quality 88) because this system's `gdk-pixbuf`
does not decode WebP, opaque PNGs were converted to JPEG for size, and PNG was
kept only where transparency had to be preserved. `assets/headers/index.tsv`
(dominant hue, saturation, lightness per file) is generated by
`tools/build_header_index.py` and is original to this repository.

**Takedown policy.** If you own the copyright to any of this artwork and do not
want it distributed here, open an issue at
<https://github.com/loonbac/loon-launch/issues> identifying the file or files,
and they will be removed promptly and without argument.
